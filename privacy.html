Privacy policy

Your files stay in your workflow.

Effective 19 October 2026. This policy describes Kamel's personal Google OAuth application used with the locally installed rclone sync program.

Data accessed and purpose

The application accesses file contents and metadata in the Google Drive account authorized by its owner. It uses this access to list, compare, copy, update, and delete files as required by the owner's configured bidirectional synchronization workflow. It is for personal use.

Storage and retention

File copies, sync state, logs, and configured backups are stored on the owner's computers and Google Drive. OAuth credentials are stored in the owner's local rclone configuration, separately from this public website. Local copies and backups remain until the owner removes them; revoking authorization does not automatically delete existing files.

Sharing

This personal workflow does not sell Drive data or send it to advertising or analytics services. Files are transferred between the owner's devices and Google Drive for synchronization and backup. Google processes data under its own privacy policy.

This information website

This static site does not include sign-in forms, analytics scripts, or a mechanism to receive Drive files or OAuth tokens. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process website visitor information under its privacy statement.

Control and revocation

The account owner can stop the local sync jobs and revoke the application's access in Google Account connections. The owner controls deletion of local and Drive copies and backups.

Contact

For questions, contact kam4eo through the project's GitHub issues. Issues are public: do not post credentials, private file contents, or sensitive filenames.
